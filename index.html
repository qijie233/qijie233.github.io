Projects
  • MetingAPI:

    A free NetEase Cloud Music VIP track parser

  • China Trip Planner:

    Give it a point A and a point B, and it lays high-speed rail / train, bus & metro, taxi, driving and walking out in one table, works out door-to-door time and cost, then recommends the fastest, cheapest and best-all-round options. It’s an AI Skill.

  • China Rail Transfer:

    An agent skill and a standalone CLI tool: give it a departure station, an arrival station and a date, and it first stitches two train segments together, then filters them one by one against your minimum transfer buffer and cross-station commute time, outputting only the itineraries you can actually catch.
