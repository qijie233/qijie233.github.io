Level V-712 | The Pure Blue World

Qijie
2024-03-30
Reflections
Backrooms, Fiction

Survival Difficulty: 0

  • Safe
  • Stable
  • Few entities

Level V-712 is the 712th level of the Backrooms V Level Group (V=Vocaloid).

Description:

This Level is an infinite layered space. The lighting here is extremely unique: both the bright areas and the dark ones are #66CCFF, and the edges of objects show a white outline. Because there is no alternation of day and night, the sky here stays in daytime all year round, which affects some wanderers' biological clocks to a certain degree.

Some of the doors in this space can be opened, and once opened they connect to other areas of this Level (connection range >= 7.12km).

This space has some stairs and ramps, connecting the floors of all sorts of buildings inside this Level that make you feel a strange sense of déjà vu.

This Level randomly spawns MC-style chests on the map, and the contents may spawn according to the following probability table:

  • Xiaolongbao (item, edible) 71.2% fixed probability of spawning 1, 2.0% base probability of spawning 2
  • Almond water 25.0% base probability of spawning 2 bottles, 50.0% base probability of spawning 3 bottles, 20.0% base probability of spawning 4 bottles
  • Disposable power bank 7.12% base probability of spawning 1
  • Broken bicycle (physical) 0.712% base probability of spawning 1
  • Random Luo Tianyi album 0.712% base probability of spawning one disc or one electronic memory stick

When there is no content in a chest and there are no people/entities within 71.2km of it, the chest's contents refresh accordingly.

The network in this Level is surprisingly good. Due to the influence of some kind of force field, any message sent inside this Level will not be received by people in the Frontroom.

Entities:

Luo Tianyi

Spawns in this Level with a 0.20120712% fixed probability, and the spawn distance is >= 712km (that is, no more will spawn within that distance).

You can feed her xiaolongbao to listen to the songs she sings. After listening to her songs, your effect hit rate will randomly increase or decrease (by at most ±2.33%), which in turn affects the base probabilities of events in this Level. In extremely rare cases you can sing along with her and obtain the achievement "Chorus Beyond the Sky"; when singing along with her there is a 7.12% base probability of randomly triggering 10 effects (including but not limited to: gaining or losing an item/Buff, etc.).

In most cases she is safe and harmless, but when she meets the tiny minority of clickbait-account owners she shows a strong desire to attack.

Her main attack method is swallowing things in big mouthfuls. Once a wanderer's item "Genius Society Common Gossip" was swallowed in one gulp by her, to the point that the wanderer suffered an unprecedented mental blow.

Bases, Outposts and Communities

Jinyiwei

A group formed by a bunch of people who like Luo Tianyi. The members of this group help each other and often share their supplies to make sure they can survive in this Level.

It's said that a clickbait-account owner who launched a verbal attack on Luo Tianyi was attacked by this group.

Entrances and Exits:

Entrances

  • Dancing "66CCFF" in Level 0 has a 71.2% base probability of entering this Level
  • Walking out through the back door in Level V-712.1 | Concert lets you enter a random area of this Level
  • Trying to swim to the bottom of the sea in the ocean of Level 7: if you meet a Luo Tianyi appearing in Imbibitor Lunae form, she will lead you to this Level (the base probability of this event is 0.0712%). If you listened to "Swimming Toward the Deep Blue Abyss" before doing this, the base probability of this event increases to 7.12%
  • Trying to Noclip into randomly spawned Luo Tianyi merchandise or posters in Level 11 has a 1.6% base probability of bringing you to this level
  • On a Linux computer in The End, giving the file "/tmp/V712intro" permission 712 lets you enter this Level
  • In The Hub, entering the door numbered #66CCFF (the fixed spawn probability of that door is 7.12%, spawn distance >= 6737151m)
  • When playing Genshin Impact, if you name the main character "Luo Tianyi" and try to glitch Xiangling's model in to replace the main character's model (as shown below), then when you click the "8"-shaped braid on her head there is a 7.12% base probability of arriving at this Level
  • If you listened to "Goodnight Candy Jar" before going to sleep, then there is a 1.6% base probability that you will enter this Level through a dream during this sleep
  • In Level V-722 | A Different Atlantis, if you try to get Haiyi to tie an "8"-shaped braid, there is a 7.12% base probability that Haiyi will lead you into this Level
  • When playing Simulated Universe: Expansion Device, before using the "Audience Device" to throw the "Die of Audience", if you listened to "Fate in the Palm", then the base probability of rolling the die face "Special · Luo Tianyi" in this run (die face effect: sucks the player in front of the screen in and teleports them to this Level) is 7.12%. When entering this Level this way, an additional "Audience Device" is generated for returning to the Simulated Universe, and the spawn distance of that "Audience Device" is >= 71.2km
  • If, after boarding The Metro's "Astral Express", you give its conductor "Pom-Pom" an "8"-shaped braid, then there is a 71.2% base probability that a jump of the "Astral Express" will bring you to this Level
  • If you watch any of Luo Tianyi's concerts on a computer in The End, there is a 7.12% base probability that you will be teleported straight to Level V-712.1 | Concert
  • After entering "Luo Tianyi" in the hidden Level Choose Your Difficulty and pressing Enter, the first time it will say "Your input is invalid, please try again." Input "Luo Tianyi" once more and press Enter and you will arrive at this Level directly
  • When listening to "The Original Heart Remains" with the "Portable Hatsune Miku-themed CD Player" in Level V-39, there is a 7.12% base probability of being teleported straight to this Level

Exits

  • When launching Genshin Impact there is a 7.0% base probability that you will see a beam of strong light taking you to Level 0 (the Origin of All Things)
  • When launching Honkai: Star Rail there is a 12.0% base probability of arriving at The Metro. The Metro arrived at this way has a 100% fixed probability of having an "Astral Express" docked in it.
  • When launching Honkai Impact 3rd there is a 2.5% base probability of arriving at the hidden Level: Elysian Realm
  • When launching Arknights there is an 8.5% base probability of arriving at Level 85
  • When launching Blue Archive there is a 0.555% base probability of arriving at Level 0.555
  • Noclipping into the floor of this Level has a 0.0711% fixed probability of entering Level V-711 | 71.1% Sweetness
  • Noclipping into a chest in this Level has a 0.6% base probability of entering Level V-712.1 | Concert

Explanation of some terms:

  • Base probability: a probability that changes under the influence of factors such as your own effect hit rate
  • Fixed probability: unlike base probability, a fixed probability is a probability that will not be affected by any factor
  • Noclip: if you've played games on the Goldsrc/Source engine, then Noclip is definitely nothing new to you. It's similar to walking through walls, except that behind the wall there may be a specific Level of the Backrooms connected to it
