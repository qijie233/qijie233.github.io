Using WeChat Input Method Again

Qijie
2024-02-23
Tech Notes
Input Method, Review

About WeChat Input Method

I’m sure everyone already knows that green little chat app that’s small and beautiful — after all, it’s a social app with hundreds of millions of users that is small and beautiful. As a newcomer, WeChat Input Method is probably something most people wouldn’t even consider; they’d still go for the old-guard Baidu IME or Sogou IME — me, Qijie, included. After all, WeChat………….WeChat is “small and beautiful”
This whole post was typed with WeChat Input Method

First Encounter with WeChat Input Method

That day I was scrolling Bilibili when I saw a Weibo blogger reviewing WeChat Input Method. Back then WeChat Input Method had just come out — probably the very first version. From the uploader’s testing it perfectly matched WeChat’s claim of being small and beautiful: the characters you typed were stored in WeChat’s local log database, and after the blogger’s nonstop testing, WeChat Input Method’s footprint had reached a dozen-plus GB. That was when I gave up on the idea of using this input method

Meeting WeChat Input Method Again

On the recommendation of a friend of mine, I said at the time that there’s nothing good about something small and beautiful, but he said it has no ads, and then told me to give it a try, so I downloaded it and had a go

Getting Started

Of course, my two main platforms are Windows and Android, so those are the two I tested on here

Windows

Let’s compare it against the national favorite, Sogou IME

The installer alone is more than twice as big, which really is hard to keep a straight face about — so they really did bring that small-and-beautiful thing over to WeChat Input Method too!

Main Features

It’s got basically everything an input method should have

Frequently Used Phrases

The most convenient feature in my opinion is frequently used phrases

This is genuinely useful for me. Putting the names I use often in there really saved me from always mistyping names or having to type them out one character at a time — total liberation!

Cross-Device

Next is the cross-device feature, which I, Qijie, personally really like. I used to think it was an exclusive feature for phones and computers of the same brand, but this time I actually got to try it, and it feels so good

Let me explain it briefly: the whole cross-device thing works without logging in, and WeChat Input Method as a whole has no login design at all — that alone beats most input methods on the market. Devices are synced with a pairing code, and once you enter it they automatically link and sync. This perfectly solves the annoying business where my computer sends a verification code, I check it on my phone, memorize it, and then type it into the computer. Now I just copy on the phone and press ctrl+v on the computer. And no need to worry about latency either — in testing, copy on the phone and paste on the computer was second-level fast, which is pretty impressive

Dark Mode

Why can’t the desktop version turn on dark mode? I really am speechless

Mobile Version

Ugh, the mobile installer is 166M?! Okay okay, as expected of an input method put out by the small-and-beautiful folks. I figured it wouldn’t be that big — I honestly didn’t expect that
Let me check how much total space it takes up now

No way, no way — total space used on the phone has reached an astonishing 0.5G. For an input method that’s already pretty huge

Main Features

It has basically all the features an input method has, same as on Windows

Vibration + Sound

This one I’m fairly happy with. Combined with the X-axis linear vibration motor that basically every phone has now, typing feels great

Plus Features

Here’s how the official description puts it

The most useful ones are spelling check and expression optimization
What just happened — don’t scold me! I’ve been through nine years of compulsory education! (doge)

As for expression optimization, it’s actually very useful at times

Ah no no, I never said I wanted to get together! Don’t just interpret what I mean however you like

Custom Toolbar

This part is very convenient, but I only put a simplified/traditional Chinese toggle in it — don’t ask me what it’s for


So those are all the features you just saw above

Handy Little Features

  • Double-tap the space bar to type a full stop
  • Automatically inserts a space between Chinese and numbers, or between Chinese and English
  • Swipe up on the delete key to clear everything you’ve typed, swipe down to restore what you just typed
  • Customizable punctuation in the sidebar
  • Automatic capitalization of the first letter (seems most input methods have this one)
Dark Mode & Skins

You all saw the screenshots above — it does have a dark mode
But it has no skins. I actually don’t mind that, because maybe I’m just a person who lacks joy in life? Anyway, I think minimal is the best-looking

Conclusion

I really do like the overall UI design on both platforms I use, but as someone who keeps dark mode on all the time, why can’t the input box in the Windows version enable dark mode? I don’t understand. And also, as an input method, why does it take up so much space? I’m a hundred and twenty percent unable to understand that. The best part though is that there are no ads and it’s clean — something most input methods in China can’t manage. I hope they get the storage footprint under better control in the future
That’s it, that’s all I’ve got
