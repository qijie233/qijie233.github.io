Can People Live the Way They Hope To?

This post is nothing but Qijie writing during an acute episode

What follows may affect your mood — please think twice before reading

I actually quite envy the people whose parents try to support them no matter what they do (illegal things aside, of course). They’re the ones who genuinely get respect from their family at home. At home, every time my parents can’t out-argue me, they bring out seniority to press me down, and the meaning is clear: here, I’m the one who decides. There’s not much I can do about it either — I just take it. Who told me to be born to them.

In middle school I was a little cutie, and I somehow even got myself a partner. Maybe it’s my own problem. I have no idea why it ended with the first one (I genuinely don’t know — she never said, and I couldn’t think of anything either). The second one, ah, the second one cheated on me. I naively thought she and I could become something, I was just too naive. Because of her, my view of love changed. Right, I’ve been in two relationships in total, and both times the other person came to me first… So my view of love turned into: one look from far away is enough. Like the sun — don’t get close. If you ask whether I like anyone right now, saying no would most likely be a lie. She probably can’t see this, so I’ll just say it out loud: XYF, I like you (covering my face). And it was also because of that second relationship that I started to become depressed. I can’t write this part; if I keep going I’ll cry again.

As the high school entrance exam got close, I was depressed to the point of death, because I was still a way off from my goal, plus my parents going on about this and that — I was right on the edge of breaking down. There was nobody at school who could listen to what was in my heart. Every day I kept myself going on self-harm; my right forearm has sixty-plus cuts on it, because I really couldn’t stand that miserable feeling anymore. At first I used something sharp; when I cut I didn’t feel much, I could only see that it was all blood. Later I tried something blunter and cut slowly. It hurt a lot, but the emotion I’d been holding down in my chest got a little smaller. In the end I got into a very ordinary high school through that exam.

At this school I wear my mask even heavier. Even if I was depressed a moment ago, if someone calls me I’ll still force out a smiling face. On the outside, I look sunny and cheerful; only I know it’s all an act, and it’s exhausting. Just now I cut two more lines into myself; seeing my arm turn red, my mood somehow got a bit better. If I’m supposed to live on in my own original way, then I’d rather just jump off the building/

Speaking of death, I still look forward to it, because living is just too tiring. Annoyed at this, annoyed at that. Look at the grades of the people in my class — I’m just a little piece of trash. Sometimes I start crying for no reason at all; there’s a particularly unbearable feeling inside me, and I can’t stop it. In class I can only secretly wipe my tears. I don’t want anyone to know what I look like now.

Why do I like the anime world? Because it’s a story, and inside a story you don’t get disturbed by the outside. I remember going to see Suzume with a classmate this year; by the end I was crying my eyes out. My classmate asked me, this one isn’t even that painful, what’s there to cry about? What he didn’t know is that I was crying about myself. Most anime stories manage to have a beautiful ending. Every time I see those endings, a feeling comes up inside me and keeps making me think: why not me, why does the world I live in have so many disappointing things in it.

There are two lines in Honkai Impact 3rd: “Fight for all the beauty in the world”, “I want to turn this imperfect story into the way we hope it to be”. After writing those two lines, I cried again. The story in HI3 really does hurt, but look at the characters in HI3 — in the end they all get a beautiful ending. Then look at me, a nobody: what can I do? I can’t do anything.

Sometimes when I look at my cat, I feel pathetic — I’m not even living as comfortably as a cat. Looking at my cat, I really want to go over and talk to him, but after all, he’s a cat; he can’t keep me company chatting.

Writing up to here, I look back at the title, and I want to say: I really don’t know what kind of person I want to become, but one thing is certain — at the very least, not the way I am now.

Living is really tiring. Living is really unbearable — it’s just me doing penance on my own.

I really want to leap down and down from the 33rd floor, so that there would never be any more troubling things at all, everything cleared out.
