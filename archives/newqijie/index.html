I Don't Want to Live for Others Anymore

Qijie
2026-03-11
Reflections

Even though I'd already suspected for a few years that I had some kind of mental illness, I never really took it seriously. Then at the end of 2025, at a mental health center near my university, I was diagnosed with severe depression and bipolar disorder

For a long time I didn't tell my friends, because I was afraid of being seen as a psycho. I'm a people-pleaser — I don't want to have a bad relationship with anyone. I'm scared, and I don't even know what I'm scared of; I'm just scared of having a bad relationship. The people who've given the most love are often the ones who've never truly felt love, and I think maybe that's my situation. I'm deeply ashamed to say the word love-starved, but I do take the initiative to love others. I'm an excellent "tree hollow" — someone to pour your secrets into — from middle school all the way to now. But honestly, the number of times I've actually told someone what's in my heart is countable on one hand. The emotional education that Chinese kids are so lacking in — I think I'm a pretty good example of that.

I'm someone who cries a lot, but the people around me can't tell. No one would cry for me, and that's why I keep crying. All the beautiful feelings I want are pinned entirely on literature and the online world — none of them have ever actually happened. Like right now: I really want a good friend, and I'd work hard to have a good relationship with them, comforting them when they're unhappy or feeling bad. I don't know whether that works either. I have been hugged before — honestly, it really felt nice. He's a middle schooler and I'm a university student; we hugged when we met up through amateur radio. It's just a special feeling.

What do the dozens of scars on my right arm amount to? Just that when I feel awful inside I can only hiss at myself and claw at myself. I know cutting my hand is useless and only hurts myself, but I can't stop. How I wish someone would come and stop me, but no one even knows I cut, and no one can put an end to my pain. Writing these things down isn't easy either — I suppose this counts as one way of telling you.

In the past month I've even started trying OD (substance abuse) — pregabalin, baclofen, dextromethorphan and the like. My sense of morality kept me from abusing them any more deeply, and right now I'm only using pregabalin; the largest single dose I've taken was 1800mg. Chemicals give me bliss cheaply and in quantity — a box of pregabalin costs only a dozen or so yuan and gives me one and a half uses. But my own values tell me that what I'm doing is wrong, and I'm actively tapering down right now. I don't recommend substance abuse to anyone.

The chief specialist at Shanghai Mental Health Center recommended that I be hospitalized, but I absolutely will not be hospitalized. As I said above, I hate the idea of being locked up as a mental patient, but the medication hasn't brought much relief either. Every special-needs appointment costs six hundred, and lately, because of the medication, I've gained over ten jin in weight, which makes me very anxious.

I already had anxiety about gender back in middle school. Yeah, that's right — in elementary and middle school, and it got noticeably stronger in high school. But I didn't dare go on HRT. I'm also in a state of anxiety disorder, and the anxiety has gotten clearly worse. As the title says, I don't want to live for other people anymore, so I chose HRT. I haven't told any of my friends, though — if I let them know they'd definitely think I've gone mad. The world still isn't very friendly toward transgender people. I haven't told my family either🍥. If someone asks me what about my family, then to hell with it — I'm not going to live for other people anymore. Last year I made a bit of money writing projects, not much, but enough to go out and travel. Looks like things are getting better, right? In reality I've just run out of options. If I spend the money and come back and the anxiety and depression haven't improved, then among the roads left open to me, I think the dead end might be one of the choices

I also wish there were a drug like the one in Oniichan wa Oshimai! — drink it, become a girl, and start life over, with friends and an older sister. I have nothing. I'm all alone. In real life, one thing I can be sure of is that I don't have any friend I'm closer to than the ones I have online. I feel awful. I hate who I am now. I envy the ones who pass — most of them already have a new life. What I envy is their friends; what I want to become is a girl; what I hate is myself and my difficulty communicating my feelings
