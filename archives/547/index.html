Rambling

When you first start using i3wm you’ll be very unused to it (especially that pile of scalp-numbing keybindings), but as you keep using it (and wear each other in), you’ll find that the more you use i3wm, the smoother it gets, and windows become easier to find — unlike traditional Windows-style stacking window managers, where one window sits on top of another until it’s hard to find anything and your blood pressure goes up.

On KDE, i3wm uses far fewer resources than KWin_x11.
