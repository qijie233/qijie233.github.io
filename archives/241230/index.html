The Last Joy of 2024 — The School's New Year Gala

Qijie
2024-12-31
Life & Misc › Reflections
Performance, Recap

School planning + preparation

At first I thought the schedule was normal, because the auditions started on the 20th, but the further it went, the more wrong it felt.

I was hosting the show, but after trying on the suit around the 10th or so, I heard nothing at all. In the end it dragged all the way to the 28th, when I was called over during the afternoon class and told: here's the script, here's the program — and as for the linking lines between acts? The hosts write them themselves. Sure, sure, write them myself? I thought I could be funny with it, but after I handed it in it got cut down mercilessly to around 50 characters. I really did the sweating-emoji thing there.

I thought things would be settled on the 28th and the show on the 30th wouldn't be a big problem. Well............. the rehearsal wasn't until the evening of the 29th, and absurdly, acts were still being added and cut that very night. Normally lots of people sign up for the various songs and dances, so the person in charge turned several submitted dances into a dance medley. Uh.... that works too? Normally announcing an act means reading out two names, but with a medley that's hard. Two of the people in the dance medley are from my class, so I just read out their two names (who doesn't have a bit of selfishness, eh)

I spent the whole evening study period on the 29th busy in the gymnasium, adding an act here, cutting an act there. The linking lines kept being revised and my head was spinning. But it ended perfectly in the end, with no major problems. Then it was just waiting for the prep work on the morning of the 30th.

Here's a look at the light show from our rehearsal — since it was late at night, it looked really striking.

Preparation

At 8:30 the next morning, all five hosts gathered at the school's Youth League committee office. I forgot to mention it above: there's a male-female pair from Senior 1 and Senior 3 (I'm in Senior 3), plus a young female teacher. The few of us made cue cards at the committee office.

After finishing and collecting the cue cards we could finally take a break; then it was into the dresses & suits to wait for the makeup artist.

Makeup

lol

I actually didn't expect the school to bring in a makeup artist specially. The girls' makeup was a bit more of a hassle; for us boys it was very simple.

At first I wasn't quite on board with this hairstyle, because it made me look a bit like Tian Yiming (no offense intended). They pushed my hair back and fixed it with hairspray.

I used to cover my forehead with bangs, so I was a bit doubtful about this hairstyle, but then the makeup artist and my co-host said you've got to try something new now and then — besides, this makes you look energetic.

Honestly I'm not that opposed to the hairstyle (being opposed wouldn't help anyway)

Lunch

In a way I never would have imagined. I thought the school would give us free lunch in the cafeteria or something; instead the school paid and someone went straight to the fast-food shop at the school gate and bought five meals back. We ate after the makeup was done, so it was a bit cold, the taste was very ordinary — not even as good as the school cafeteria — and each of the five meals had different dishes.

This photo is the one I ate

Milktea

Eheh, the host teacher is so nice — she ordered each of us a cup of Chagee. I went to the school gate to pick up the delivery. In theory the school doesn't allow delivery food, but a teacher on my side is outside the rules (side-eye grin).

Thanks again, teacher

This cup of milk tea was a marathon too — I was still drinking it at the halftime of the afternoon show (facepalm laugh)

A little Easter egg I found in the committee office

No way, no way. Where am I supposed to find someone who both draws anime and plays BA?

As a total art disaster myself, I'm just kneeling in admiration

The show!!

I really don't like wearing leather shoes — they probably don't fit quite right; anyway, the feeling is truly hard to put into words

With several thousand people watching from below, it would be fake to say I wasn't nervous. When I got on stage and that spotlight hit my body, I suddenly felt fired up — and what replaced the nerves was the smile on my face.

The opening ceremony turned out to be just simple announcements, so I slipped away from backstage to go have fun. The most absurd part: backstage was colder than the main hall, and there's no AC backstage. In Jiangnan weather, wearing just a thermal top under a suit is still a bit cold; throwing a down jacket on over it helps a little.

Otaku dance!!

Sorry for Being So Cute!

Thank you, otaku dance — by the time you came on I had already lost my mind!!!

Whoever among the school leaders approved the otaku dance for the review, really, I could cry. Because for the sports meet, my co-host and I went to the school committee secretary's office and went head-to-head with him, and still couldn't win permission to cosplay at the sports meet. Numb.

God knows how excited I was when the otaku dance started (from watching anime, from playing in the two-dimensional world, from wandering around anime conventions)

Video attached. The unhinged laughter at the beginning is mine. The committee teacher saw how excited I was and teased: "Oh! So this is what you like!" Help, please don't say it, I'll cover the awkwardness with my unhinged laughter 2333333
