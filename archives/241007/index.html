Hangzhou!

Qijie
2024-10-07
Life & Misc
Daily Life, Snapshots, Travel

The Painful Part

Why didn't I get a CP30 ticket!!

I, the uploader, went to great lengths to grab a CP31 ticket!

If only I hadn't tried to get tickets on CPP. Their app's servers are so terrible they just freeze (I was fuming), so I didn't get one at all. Bilibili's Member Mall could have gotten me a ticket and I didn't go (), so in the end no CP30 for me

The high-speed rail ticket to Hangzhou was grabbed 15 days before the con opened (hard to get during the National Day holiday!). What left me speechless is that con tickets only went on sale three days before, which had me silently cursing the CP organizers

Then again, the (train) ticket was already bought, and refunding it costs a fee, so off I went! (I hadn't done any planning at all)

A special-forces style trip — go and come back the same day — just like going to BilibiliWorld: alone (seriously, nobody came with me)

Off I Go!

Qijie set off from Nanjing South Railway Station

This time I chose to get up early, rather than doing what I used to do for Shanghai trips — getting to the station the night before to wait for a train in the small hours

I got to Nanjing South early, waited twenty-something minutes, and then boarded the high-speed train to Hangzhou

The morning sun looked pretty nice too; I didn't really take the photo seriously, so it doesn't look that great

I got a window seat and dozed for half an hour. Why only half an hour? Not that I didn't want to sleep, but that I genuinely couldn't. Sleepy? Yes! Could I sleep? No! So I had to pull out my phone and watch anime — it was <Alya Sometimes Hides Her Feelings in Russian> and it was pretty good. Watching it, Qijie kept breaking into an auntie smile, then I'd remember there were people beside me and force it down. I kept watching on the Hangzhou metro too

Arrival!

As we got into downtown Hangzhou there were lots of towers, and looking closely — oh, isn't that NetEase HQ? But the train was too fast and I didn't get a photo in time qaq

After about an hour on the high-speed train, I finally arrived!! Hangzhou East Railway Station!

This was also my first time in Hangzhou. Since CP30 was off the table, I might as well have some fun in Hangzhou

Getting off the train and into the metro, I found this coser quite interesting

Too bad I don't watch Hazbin Hotel, otherwise I'd have gone over and said: oh, you play Genshin too? (not really)

Then I took the metro to the Hangzhou Grand Convention and Exhibition Center to see whether there was any off-site event to watch, and found that the dirt outside the convention center was wet!! It sticks to your shoes. Ugh, seriously. And then I realized: if there were an off-site event in a place like this, that would really be something (─.─|||. Then I took the metro back, wasting the fare for nothing, and moved on to the main quest: seeing West Lake

West Lake?

On the metro I thought of the line “West Lake may be compared to Xizi — lovely whether lightly made up or richly adorned.” That's pretty much my impression of West Lake, plus the scenery on the back of the 10-yuan note

It's always lovely in your head. Yeah, reality likes to smack you in the face. Fine, lots of people, that's whatever............ but is this really......... West Lake? Good-looking?

Honestly speaking, this scenery doesn't even feel as good as my good old Nanjing. Still a ton of people. I just followed the crowd — not because I wanted to walk or look, but because there were so many people there was no way to turn back. Well, after a lap around, my stomach was a bit hungry since breakfast was just a slice of bread. I saw someone selling grilled sausages and my mouth felt lonely. Who cares if it's a starch sausage — it smelled good. 10RMB a stick, so expensive! Bought it! Because I wanted it (not really) — but it really did smell amazing at the time

Melting Snow on the Broken Bridge

Man, the name is famous as anything. In reality, though

There were even more people further along, so no more photos (CDN and OSS both cost money). In one sentence: it didn't live up to expectations

If you insist on asking what one trip to West Lake brought me, all I can say is: I've been to West Lake now — just one more thing to talk about in front of others. I honestly didn't feel it was beautiful

Squirrel 🐿

Ehehe, there are cute little squirrels by West Lake! I really want to scoop one up and take it home

Noon

For the lunch question I went to the 7-Eleven near West Lake and bought a beef skewer. Tasty! (Photo taken inside KFC)

It really was tasty. Then I had lunch inside KFC — the prices are just a tiny bit expensive, like a hundred million times. I always thought KFC prices were the same nationwide 555555 (the cheapest thing to eat in Shanghai is KFC)

It's fine, I comforted myself: you can't go out and not spend money, right? At noon I ate KFC while scrolling Bilibili, looking up which anime pilgrimage spots Hangzhou has. You don't know until you look — Hangzhou's ACG and merch economy is this developed! Lucky me! ✨Suddenly I felt fired up for the afternoon

I saw something hilarious in the KFC by West Lake: an elementary schooler actually pulled out homework in there? Out of the backpack, and just started doing homework right there???

The Official Apple Store

After finishing KFC I wandered into the Apple store. The Apple store is right as you come out of one of the exits of Longxiangqiao Station (also the metro station by West Lake). People were already queuing before it opened in the morning

I went in and casually looked at the latest iPhone 16, MacBooks and the like. The prices have always been something I can only dream of, so I just looked around

Main Quest

Why call it the main quest? Mostly because the whole original reason for coming to Hangzhou was to attend the anime event — except I didn't get a ticket ()

Now all three shopping districts near West Lake have a whole floor set aside for ACG stuff! Yay!

Look at the map, they're all around! Hubin 88, Gonglian CC, and in77

Before browsing I bought a cup of Chagee to fill my lonely mouth. Why Chagee, you ask? Because Meituan's homepage just happened to push me a Chagee "Sharpshooter" coupon — large size, 22 discounted to 18

Gonglian CC has an entire floor selling merch, 5 stars ++++. The foot traffic really is heavy, but not quite crowded yet

Hahahaha, even though this is very serious, it's just so funny

While queuing to pay I heard someone chatting about Bocchi the Rock and couldn't help joining in. Well, I was still wondering whether I'd overstepped, and the other person just said: oh, you too. Hahahaha, in that instant it felt like we weren't strangers anymore. The merch was bought at the shop in the photo above

Don't Be a Gambler

Honestly, I've actually done very little of this pulling cards out of blind bags thing before. To pull one Sparkle laser ticket from the Honkai: Star Rail “Fable of the Stars” series, it really hurt my wallet so much. How many times have I said "last time", but I really can't help it!!! Every shop I walk into sells them! I truly can't resist. And the result....... "last time" for the hundred-millionth time. Luckily it came out on the seventh card — 9.9 each, 70 in total. What came out: Himeko, Topaz, Dr. Ratio, Luocha, Imbibitor Lunae, Argenti, Sparkle!! Seven cards bought from three shops, the last one twice. I'm also kind of glad — good thing it came out on the second try, otherwise there'd be another hundred million times..........

Have a look at the chat log with my friend (click to enlarge)

Otaku Dance

Ehehe, otaku dance is forever my favorite

Here's a video to watch — just photos gets boring

And now Gonglian CC's fourth floor — I really love lively scenes

That couple playing maimai are so made for each other ahhhhhhhh, help, I'm so jealous — they even hug after finishing a round. So sweet

The little girl up on the dance machine is insanely 🐂🍺 — her completion rate is in the nineties almost every time. And then look at stiff-limbed me..............

Sunset & Afterglow

Photo flood warning!!!

I really, really love this stretch of sunset. Seeing that everyone around me has their own family, friends or partner beside them, I can't help feeling a bit forlorn. I want to say to them too: look, this sunset is really beautiful!

I checked the time again — two hours left before I'd take the high-speed train home, and I felt a bit forlorn again. The three-day holiday was about to be gone, and I'd be back to my own life and studies

I walked through the three shopping districts once more. This time I didn't buy any more merch — I just wanted to feel what it's like to be among people once more. Everyone was pretty happy, picking their favorite merch off the shelves, smiling and cheerful, and I felt a little envious, because I'd be heading back soon. I felt a bit like crying

I have to roast myself here..........I didn't take the F1 route to the metro station, but I did go into the night market and buy two ham sausages — I really was hungry. The night market was packed too, people right up against each other, and carrying a bag was really inconvenient. But the sausage smelled so good, even if it's obviously made of starch, and it wasn't as tasty as the one by West Lake

In the end I was going to go down into the metro from street level, but a lot of entrances had been changed to exit-only, because there really were way way way way way too many people — I got squeezed half to death........Anyway, here's the afterglow I caught in the sea of people

Hey!!! Gonglian CC's F1 has restaurants, and the smell made me hungry again. I couldn't be bothered to check whether this trip had blown its budget either (I usually skip proper meals when I go out, to save money), so I just ordered a beef fried rice. 35RMB felt expensive to me too, but I'd eat first and worry later. Then I found out........there was nowhere to sit down and eat. Even though the whole B1 floor was all places selling food, none of them had anywhere to sit and eat; only the central dining area on F1 had seats. So I slowly squatted there waiting and waiting. Sometimes I couldn't even tell whether someone was about to leave, and just let a seat get taken by someone else for nothing. In the end, more than ten minutes after the fried rice was ready, I managed to find a seat — it really wasn't easy

I ate while scrolling Bilibili, and then suddenly realized? It seems like it's not enough?! There's less than an hour before the train departs! Now I really panicked — otherwise getting home today would be a problem, mainly because the high-speed rail tickets back to Nanjing tomorrow would definitely be sold out during the National Day holiday, no need to even think about it! With this much foot traffic right now, that's a problem! After eating two-thirds of the fried rice I hurriedly left this hard-won, precious seat!

The crowd on Line 1 at Longxiangqiao Station, as shown

Racing for the Train

According to the DiTieTong metro transfer query app, the fastest metro route takes about 34 minutes from Longxiangqiao Station to Hangzhou West Railway Station, and I had only fifty minutes left, which also had to cover the metro transfer and waiting time. The transfer was at West Lake Cultural Square Station, and my heart sank as soon as I saw it: it's a major-station interchange between Line 1Line 3Line 19. I started cursing in my head — probably another sprint. And that's exactly what happened: when we reached the interchange, I was the first one out of the carriage and started sprinting, and it took two minutes to reach the right platform door. Luckily the train arrived after a minute of waiting. Phew

At 19:32 my metro was one stop from Hangzhou West Station; at 19:44 the train stops checking tickets. In between I still had to go through the following:

  • An absurdly long transfer from the metro to the high-speed rail station

  • High-speed rail security check

  • Sprinting inside the station

  • Finding the ticket gate

  • Finding the carriage markers

And I successfully made it! The photo below is me with a coser inside the metro station at Hangzhou West Railway Station, hehe. I love this Chisato so much!! She gave me a Lycoris Recoil card as a freebie, with "stay happy every day" written on it in marker ^^

Goodbye, Hangzhou

At first I thought that Chisato above and I were just ships passing in the night; what I never expected was that our fate went further than that!

On the train I found out we were on the same service, and then she sat down right next to me!! Holy crap, seriously, happiness came so suddenly — what does it feel like to see your oshi sitting next to you?! I was so happy. After boarding we chatted a bit, then she started sleeping — running around a con all day really is tiring. During May Day, on the metro back to the station after the Star Rail carnival, I fell asleep and leaned onto an older guy next to me; thank goodness he woke me up, otherwise it would have been really awkward (though it was already very awkward when he woke me).

I was so incredibly happy at that moment, hahahaha. I kept sneaking glances at her; her phone wallpaper was Shen Xinghui

Later, after she woke up, she shared some Kuromi collaboration snacks she'd bought at the con with me and complained to me that they were too sweet — all she wanted was the little card inside — then gave me a few Lycoris Recoil cards, saying they were leftovers from the con. Then I asked her which station she was getting off at — hey! She was getting off at Nanjing South too. That's what fate between people is, I guess. Right before we got off I finally asked her, in a tiny voice, whether I could add her, and in the end we added each other on QQ! Yay!

When we reached the station and were getting ready to get off, she held me back and said not to rush, it's crowded right now (I was in the window seat). Help, blessed again!

Leaving the station, she took the metro home while I was going to grab a taxi. At the parting, I sent her a message on QQ: bye bye, coser!

And just like that, my one day in Hangzhou was over. It went by so fast
