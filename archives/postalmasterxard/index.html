PSBC Mastercard | The International Card Most Worth Having

Qijie
2025-05-30
Life & Misc › Qijie's Ramblings
Bank Card, Economy, Mastercard, Society

Preface

Even so, the blogger already owns a Bank of China Winter Olympics VISA card, and there aren't that many restrictions on using it, so there's plenty of room to work with
But!!!!! The only bad part is that you need a Class I account to buy foreign exchange and then transfer it into this card, which is a real hassle. Every time you buy or settle foreign exchange, the FX purchase/settlement application form hard-stuns you for 30 seconds 😭
Until I saw this card that PSBC released earlier: it supports RMB settlement both domestically and overseas, and when foreign currency is settled in RMB it doesn't eat into your personal convenient FX quota (even though the fifty thousand US dollars annual per-person limit is something I'd never use up anyway)

But I'd always wanted to apply and always forgot, so it just sat on the back burner. And the most important point: it needs a 25 RMB card production fee. That's right, you have to pay to apply. But then again, after you pay the 25 RMB and apply, it gives you back three 10 RMB WeChat vouchers, usable on orders over 10.01. And later, after activation, I linked Alipay, and Alipay threw in a 10 RMB voucher too, four in total, 40 RMB. They can be deducted when you spend with the PSBC debit card. Ehehe.

Activation

You'll get the card in about 4-5 days; bring your ID card to a branch and activate it there. Here I have to complain about the PSBC staff.
They opened a Class II account for me. I asked why they wouldn't open a Class I account, and they said I'm a student, too young, they don't open Class I. I asked why Bank of China was able to open a Class I for me, and then they acted like they were facing a mortal enemy — no, seriously, is having a card at another bank not allowed either? Making a fuss right here? Later I just said I had a card at Bank of China and said nothing else. In total I have

  • Bilibili 2233 wedding-dress co-branded China Merchants Bank card

  • Honkai: Star Rail Topaz co-branded CITIC Bank card

  • Bank of China Bilibili 2233 co-branded card

  • Bank of China Winter Olympics co-branded VISA card

  • China Guangfa Bank Lucky American Express card

  • China Guangfa Bank "Carp Leaping the Dragon Gate" Mastercard

Ugh, I give up. They said my transaction volume isn't big enough to open a Class I card. Well, I don't have a mortgage or a car loan, so of course I don't have big transaction volume, and for that they won't open a Class I for me. I said I need a Class I account to buy foreign exchange; they looked into it for ages, I explained for ages, and they still wouldn't open one. In the branch I just had to accept my bad luck, and finally I gave in: fine, just open the Class II then. I'm taking this card home to enshrine it as a collectible.

And it gets more ridiculous: five transactions per day, 20 a month, 100 a year. I asked at the time whether spending money from the card via WeChat Pay counts, and they said yes. I was even more speechless. This is a person here, only allowed to spend money 20 times a month. Are you treating your customers like 🐶? I was completely speechless all over again.

But I was speechless the whole way home........ because I have Class I cards at every major bank. I'm someone who doesn't even have 5,000 in my accounts, and they said they're worried I'll launder money??? Oh my goodness, they think far too highly of me. I'm so broke I'm about to eat dirt.

After I got home, I opened the PSBC mobile banking app and bound the card there. And then what did I see? Hey! Guess what, I saw an "upgrade to Class I" option! One tap, then a face scan to verify identity, and the card was a Class I card. My feelings at that moment were extremely complicated: something the branch absolutely refused to do, I could accomplish myself online with one tap. When a person is utterly speechless, they really do laugh. What's even more absurd: that five-transactions-a-day limit I mentioned is the limit for transfers between bank cards; Alipay and those quick payments fall under a separate section. I tapped "fill in" and the recommended limits scared me:

  • Quick payment daily cumulative limit 499,999; daily cumulative transaction count 10,000

  • Quick payment monthly cumulative limit 5,000,000; monthly cumulative transaction count 50,000

  • Quick payment annual cumulative limit 15,000,000; annual cumulative transaction count 150,000

I'm done. I now seriously suspect the tellers at the branch went on duty without any training. Those two things broke me completely.

Hands-on Experience

So far my attempt to bind PayPal failed, showing that it was declined by the issuing institution. My Winter Olympics VISA can be bound, though that might be because of my China-region account.

Binding it to UnionPay QuickPass failed too — right, QuickPass says this type of card isn't supported yet. Maybe because this is a Mastercard? Not UnionPay.

Topping up Honkai: Star Rail on the international server worked fine; it settles in Japanese yen, and with only RMB in the card it still paid perfectly; the hold ratio is 105%

Binding it to porkbun to buy a domain came with a small hiccup, which was resolved later. The first time there was only RMB in the card, and both binding attempts failed; after I bought 1 USD of foreign exchange and put it in the card, binding, paying and buying the domain all worked normally.

Whether binding or spending, you need to complete a second verification with a Postal Savings Bank verification code before the payment goes through.
