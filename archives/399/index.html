Why You Can't Aim When You First Start Playing CS2

Qijie
2023-09-17
CSGO

Understanding CS2’s Bullet Trajectory Mechanics

The gap between CS2’s bullet trajectories and CS 1.6’s is like night and day!
Right now CS2 feels not far off from 1.6, and CS2 gets updated very frequently.

Compared with CS1.6 — the following is just my personal feeling:

CS1.6’s weapon recoil is much smaller than CS2’s.

CS1.6’s bullet trajectories had several random templates:

For example, the AK had roughly four spray patterns:

The red circle means keeping the crosshair still

The Desert Eagle’s recoil is clearly much smaller than in 2.

But by CS2, the AK’s trajectory is fixed to the second one in the picture above, and the recoil is increased (about twice that of 1.6).

Likewise, in CS2 the Desert Eagle’s recoil is such that, apart from point-blank range, spraying really does send your aim up into the sky, and the demand for counter-strafing is slightly stricter than in 1.6.

Never Even Played CS1.6? Look Here

The author has learned from the people around him that some people can’t tell friend from foe. (fog) I suggest letting them know that teammates have an “inverted triangle” marker above their heads (on by default).

In CS2, when you put your crosshair on the enemy’s head and press the left mouse button while properly counter-strafed, you hit 99.99% of the time.

Yet when you run and gun (or even jump and shoot), rifles like the AK and M4 drift noticeably more. When you first start playing CS2, don’t rush into practicing the AK and M4 — get your basic aiming and positioning down first, then move on to the advanced stuff.

“Schrödinger’s Bullet” (fog)

When you aim at the enemy and press the left mouse button, at that moment the bullet is in a “superposition” of hitting and missing,

and at that point, hitting becomes a matter of probability. So if you can’t aim, just shoot more! Overwhelm probability with quantity!! (fog)

Start with the ones that have plenty of bullets. Here I recommend starting out with the XM1014 (auto-shotgun), P90, Negev and the like:

XM1014

7/32 (current/spare) ammo capacity, a “god-tier weapon” at close to mid range. Because it’s a shotgun, one shot fires more than 10 pellets (each pellet does low damage). Shotgun one guy down and that’s +900$ , three and that’s +2700$, plus the round-win bonus, which is enough to buy an AWP and full armor.

This gun can be used while running, and even while jumping.

Point-blank: one shot to the head. Up close: two shots to the body. Mid range: play for chip damage (grind down the enemy’s health).

Making good use of cover can achieve astonishing results.

But you need to have the rhythm down.

P90

50/100 ammo capacity, a rather special SMG. Spraying down one enemy only earns 300$. You absolutely must run and gun!

Generally speaking, P90, Rush B, aim at the chest not the head — first time awkward, second time smooth, third and fourth time you’re Global Elite! (uh)

This gun somewhat tests your tracking ability (a basic skill; if you ask, the answer is practice more).

Gameplay demo:

Negev

150/300 ammo capacity, and from the 10th bullet onward the spray is almost on a single point. This gun can’t be used while running; after you crouch the spray is even steadier.

As long as you hold the second angle (a position where you won’t meet the enemy right away) you’re basically “invincible” (an AK can still one-tap your head, though…)

This gun really tests spray transfer (also a basic skill).

Practice Methods

For newbies, start getting a feel for these guns in “Practice with Bots”. Once you’ve found at least two pistols and two non-pistol weapons that feel comfortable in your hands, you can go try your hand in Casual mode in official matchmaking.

Note: newbies, don't touch the Desert Eagle or the SSG 08 (bird sniper)! Otherwise no matter how you shoot you won't hit anything, or if you do hit them, they don't die and then you get one-tapped in the head.
The in-CS name Vsinger_Luo Tianyi in the video has nothing to do with the official side — that is, the Henian official isn't on the Steam platform — it's purely a joke!

Huh? You want to play the Desert Eagle? Okay okay, the Nova costs 1050, so why not spend 350 to upgrade the Desert Eagle into one with a better one-tap feel and more bullets, though you won’t be able to tap enemies very far away? Doesn’t that sound great? Cheap goods are never good (

For those with an FPS background, you should practice finding the rhythm of counter-strafing and the feel of tapping with the AK and M4; you can go look for it in Aimbotz.

Once you’ve found the feel, you need to deepen your understanding of the gun, which means playing more.

Besides aim, you also need to have an understanding of the map, use cover, and create advantages.

Afterword

Practicing your aim is not the work of a single day; it’s a gradual process.

Remember: your two hands are there to serve you for your whole life, not for CS2.
